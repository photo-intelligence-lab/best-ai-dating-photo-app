## Three things that matter more than price

- **It has to look like you on the date.** Apps that train a model on 15-20 of your own selfies beat one-photo face swaps on likeness.
- **Variety.** Hinge shows six photos. Six studio portraits read as a fake account: you want a street, a dinner, a trip.
- **No monthly bill.** You need one strong set and the odd refresh, not a content subscription.
